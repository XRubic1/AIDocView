# Why choose Credyx over a factoring company?

Marketing messaging — **education / offer**, not an attack on factors.

**Positioning:** A factor collects your invoice because **they own it** — you're paying for a cash advance. Credyx works your A/R **alongside you** because **we work for you** — you're paying for a back office, and your invoices stay yours. Many carriers use **both**.

**The honest premise (never skip this):** Good factoring companies also verify paperwork, chase missing docs, and run collections — some do it well. We do **not** win by claiming they don't do the work. We win on **what you're paying for** and **who the work serves**.

## 2. Core pitch (carrier-facing)

### The core choice: what are you paying for?

- With a **factor**, you're paying for **cash today**. The discount rate, reserves, minimums, and term commitments are the price of an advance — on every load, whether or not you needed one. The back office comes bundled, priced like financing.
- With **Credyx**, you're paying for **the back office only** — a small service fee, billed when invoices are collected. No advance, so no financing markup, no reserves, no chargebacks, nothing to unwind.

If you don't need the advance on most loads, factoring means **paying financing prices for clerical work**. On trucking margins, a few percent of gross is a big share of your net.

### They work for their invoice. We work for you.

A factor's collections protect **their receivable** — you often see little more than funded / not funded / chargeback. Credyx runs your A/R **with you**:

- We tell you exactly what's missing — you send it, we push the packet with the broker
- Shared status on every invoice: holds, disputes, docs, aging — in your portal
- Clear asks and quick fixes keep paperwork and packets **moving**, instead of stalling for weeks on a missing BOL

*(Never say "get paid faster." Say: keep packets moving, resolve holds sooner, paid on time.)*

### You keep your invoices and the money path

- We don't buy receivables — brokers pay **you** directly
- Credyx never touches the freight payment; we bill you separately for Credyx fees
- No NOA assigning your A/R to a funder, no buyout to unwind if you ever leave

### Honest cost shape — pay when collected

- **No upfront Credyx payment** for the collection fee
- Credyx takes its fee when the invoice is **collected and paid** (confirmed collections)
- Uncollected balances do not generate a Credyx % fee
- **No hidden fees** — transparent schedule, agreed at onboarding

### No chargeback risk

With recourse factoring, a broker that doesn't pay becomes a clawback plus fees — you carried the risk anyway but paid factoring prices for it. With Credyx there was never an advance to claw back.

### Works with factoring — fee goes down when you use both

If you **do** need cash flow, keep your factor. We offer **full integration with factoring companies**:

- Packets / NOA / remit paths stay coordinated
- Credyx marks the account as factoring-related (NOA on file)
- Your **Credyx collection rate is reduced** vs standard A/R-only rates (see Fee Schedule)
- A **factor advance** is not a Credyx collection event — we do **not** charge Credyx's % because "the factor paid you." We bill on eligible **confirmed collections**, at the **reduced factoring-related rate** when both are in play

## 4. Objection handlers

### "My factor already does collections and checks my paperwork."

Right — for the invoice **they bought**. That work protects their receivable, and you're paying financing prices for it. Credyx does that work **for you**: your invoices, your money path, full visibility, and we work alongside you to clear missing docs and holds so packets keep moving. Smaller fee, billed when collected.

### "I already have a factor."

Keep them for cash flow if you need advances. Add Credyx so you see every invoice end-to-end — holds, docs, status — and have a team working your A/R with you. **Your Credyx fee is lower** when factoring is on file.

### "Isn't this the same as factoring?"

No. A factor's product is the advance — the ops come bundled at financing prices. Credyx's product **is** the ops: docs → verified → invoiced → follow-up → **paid on time**. We don't buy your freight bills, so you keep control and pay a service fee instead of a discount.

### "Will Credyx take money from my broker remittance?"

No. Brokers pay **you** (or your factor under a valid NOA). Credyx bills **you** separately for Credyx fees — we do not intercept freight payments.

### "What if the factor pays me first?"

A factor advance is their product. Credyx does **not** treat that advance as our collection event. Credyx fees apply to **eligible confirmed collections** on invoices we handle — at the **reduced factoring-related rate** when you use both.

### "What do I get that my factor doesn't give me?"

A team that answers to **you**. Full invoice insight, document trail, hold/dispute visibility, broker follow-up as your A/R department, and clear asks when something's missing — not just a funded/not-funded status. And your invoices never leave your hands.
