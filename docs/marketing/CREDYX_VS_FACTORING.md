Marketing messaging — **education / offer**, not an attack on factors.

**Positioning:** Credyx is your **A/R back office** — paperwork, invoicing, follow-up, and payment tracking **on time**. Factoring is **cash against invoices**. Different jobs. Many carriers use **both**.

## 2. Core pitch (carrier-facing)

### Full insight and control

Credyx gives you **full insight and control** over your invoices:

- See **full invoice details** — status, docs, holds, disputes, aging
- **No hidden fees** — transparent Credyx pricing; you know what you pay and why
- One easy platform with everything your **back office** needs in one place

### Smaller fee than typical factoring

Credyx’s A/R fee is a **small percentage of collected volume** — typically much lower than factoring discount rates — because we are **not** buying your receivables. We run the back office so invoices get submitted clean, chased when stuck, and tracked to paid.

*(Never say “get paid faster.” Prefer: visibility, control, paid on time, lower Credyx fee vs typical factor cost.)*

### Works with factoring — fee goes down when you use both

We offer **full integration with factoring companies**. If you decide to use **Credyx + a factor**:

- Packets / NOA / remit paths stay coordinated
- Credyx marks the account as factoring-related (NOA on file)
- Your **Credyx collection rate is reduced** vs standard A/R-only rates (see Fee Schedule)

### You pay Credyx when invoices are collected — not upfront

- **No upfront Credyx payment** for the collection fee
- Credyx takes its fee when the invoice is **collected and paid** (confirmed collections)
- Uncollected balances do not generate a Credyx % fee
- A **factor advance** is not the same as broker collection through Credyx — we **do not charge Credyx’s collection fee on “the factor paid you an advance.”** We bill on eligible **confirmed collections** for invoices handled through Credyx, at the **factoring-related (reduced) rate** when both are in play

## 4. Objection handlers

### “I already have a factor.”

Good. Keep them for cash flow if you need advances. Add Credyx so paperwork is verified, brokers are invoiced correctly, holds get followed up, and you still see every invoice end-to-end. **Your Credyx fee is lower** when factoring is on file.

### “Isn’t this the same as factoring?”

No. Factors fund invoices. Credyx **manages** invoices — docs → verified → invoiced → follow-up → **paid on time**. We don’t buy your freight bills.

### “Will Credyx take money from my broker remittance?”

No. Brokers pay **you** (or your factor under a valid NOA). Credyx bills **you** separately for Credyx fees — we do not intercept freight payments.

### “What if the factor pays me first?”

A factor advance is their product. Credyx does **not** treat that advance as our collection event. Credyx fees apply to **eligible confirmed collections** on invoices we handle — and at the **reduced factoring-related rate** when you use both.

### “What do I get that my factor doesn’t give me?”

Full invoice insight, document trail, hold/dispute visibility, broker follow-up as your A/R team, and a portal built for carrier back-office — not only funding status.
