## Correct process copy (for edits / new process posts)

**Five steps**

1. **Docs received** — Carrier submits paperwork to Credyx  
2. **Paperwork verified** — We verify documents so nothing gets missed  
3. **Invoice submitted** — We submit invoice (PPW) to the broker  
4. **Broker follow-up** — We follow up, resolve issues, keep you informed  
5. **Payment received** — We confirm payment when it arrives — on time  

**Invoice card stepper:** Submitted → Verified → Invoiced → Paid  

**Headline options:** *From paperwork… to payment.* · *Every step tracked. Every update visible.*  
(Avoid “From delivery… to payment” — implies we own delivery.)
