## 1. What Credyx is (and is not)

Credyx provides accounts receivable (“**A/R**”) and back-office support for trucking carriers through the Credyx portal and related services (the “**Services**”), including receiving and verifying paperwork, preparing and submitting invoice packets to brokers/payors, following up on holds and disputes, tracking payment status, and billing Client for Credyx fees.

**Credyx is not:**

- a factoring company, lender, or cash-advance provider;
- a purchaser or assignee of Client’s freight invoices (unless Client separately factors with a third party);
- a freight broker; or
- a field service that confirms load delivery or captures POD as a Credyx workflow step.

Credyx does **not** receive or retain freight payments from brokers on Client’s behalf. Brokers/payors are directed to pay **Client**. Credyx’s compensation is limited to **fees charged to Client** under Exhibit A for eligible activity.

Marketing phrases such as “paid on time” describe the service goal of visibility and follow-up; they are **not** a guarantee of any specific payment date, amount, or broker conduct.

## 3. Service plans

Client enrolls in one of the following (as selected at enrollment or later changed in writing / by Credyx admin with Client’s agreement):

### 3.1 Self-Service

### 3.2 Managed

Credyx performs day-to-day A/R operations for Client (batch/document processing, invoice submission to brokers, follow-up, and related collections activity) using the portal, subject to Client providing complete and accurate documents and timely responses.

## 5. Document handling (critical)

### 5.1 Source documents — no content modification

Credyx does **not** modify the substantive content of Rate Confirmations, Bills of Lading, proofs of delivery, or other Client-submitted **source documents**.

Automated or AI-assisted tools may be used to:

- review, classify, and extract data from documents; and/or
- enhance documents for **readability** (for example image clarity),

but such tools will **not** be used to change the substantive content of those source documents.

### 5.2 Invoices — billing accuracy only

Credyx may prepare, edit, or adjust **invoices** solely to ensure correct billing information for submission to brokers/payors (for example amounts, references, dates, and required billing fields consistent with Client’s paperwork and instructions).

### 5.3 Remittance and banking materials

Credyx does **not** edit or change Client remittance instructions, bank letters, or voided checks provided by Client.

Where appropriate for an invoice packet, Credyx may **include** Client’s bank letter and/or voided check (or other Client-approved remittance evidence on file) so that brokers/payors direct payment to **Client**, not to Credyx.

### 5.4 No broker remittance to Credyx

Credyx does not take freight payment from brokers. Credyx invoices **Client** for Credyx fees on activity that is eligible under Exhibit A.

### 5.5 Broker / payor due dates (freight invoices)

Payment terms for freight invoices submitted to brokers or payors are **not** a single fixed period (such as “Net 30”) for every broker. Each broker or payor may have its own due date or payment terms as recorded in Credyx for that relationship (for example 30, 45, or 60 days, or another term). Credyx tracks and follows up using the due dates configured in the system for that broker/payor and invoice. Exhibit A’s payment timelines apply only to **Credyx fee bills** owed by Client to Credyx.

## 8. Collections, claims, bond assistance, and force-close

### 8.1 No guarantee of broker payment; Credyx not liable for unpaid freight

Client acknowledges that brokers and payors sometimes delay payment, dispute invoices, short-pay, or refuse to pay for reasons outside Credyx's control (including load claims, paperwork disputes, credit issues, or unwillingness to pay).

**Credyx is not liable** for:

- unpaid or late freight amounts owed by any broker or payor;
- load claims, cargo claims, detention, accessorial disputes, or other freight claims between Client and a broker, shipper, or insurer;
- a broker's or payor's refusal or failure to pay; or
- the outcome of any bond claim, lawsuit, or collection action Client pursues.

Credyx's role is A/R and back-office support. Credyx does **not** guarantee that any invoice will be paid, paid in full, or paid by any date.

### 8.2 Collection and claim-resolution efforts

For invoices handled under the Services (especially **Managed**), Credyx will use commercially reasonable efforts — and will work diligently and in good faith — to pursue payment and to help resolve holds, document issues, and payment disputes with brokers/payors, including follow-up and status tracking in the portal. "Commercially reasonable efforts" means Credyx will do what is practical and customary for an A/R back office given the facts, documents, and broker responses available; it is **not** a promise of successful collection or claim recovery.

### 8.3 Broker bond claims — assistance only

If a broker is not paying and Client wants to pursue a claim against a broker's bond (or similar surety):

- Credyx **may assist** Client by helping gather invoice/packet history, correspondence, and status information available in Credyx, and by providing reasonable guidance on typical next steps;
- Credyx will **not** file the bond claim for Client, will **not** act as Client's attorney or claims agent for bond filings, and will **not** be responsible for deadlines, forms, or outcomes of any bond claim;
- Client (or Client's counsel/agent) remains solely responsible for deciding whether to file, preparing and filing the claim, and all related costs.

### 8.4 Force-close (Managed plan only)

**Eligibility.** An invoice is eligible for force-close when all of the following are true:

1. Client is on the **Managed** plan (force-close does not apply to Self-Service);
2. The invoice is an open Credyx-handled freight invoice that is not already paid or written off; and
3. The invoice is at least **ninety (90) days** past its expected pay / due timing as tracked in Credyx (i.e., **90+ days outstanding** under Credyx's A/R policy), after Credyx has attempted collection follow-up appropriate to the circumstances.

**Right to force-close.** If an invoice is eligible, Credyx **may**, at Credyx's discretion, force-close that invoice to close out aged A/R Credyx has worked. Force-close is an operational status in Credyx; it does **not** waive Client's rights against the broker/payor, and it does **not** make Credyx liable for the unpaid freight.

**Fees.** If Credyx force-closes an eligible invoice, agreed Credyx fees may apply as described in Exhibit A (so collection work is not zero-fee solely because the broker never paid).

**Self-Service.** Self-Service clients manage their own aged invoices (for example by posting payment or requesting write-off per Credyx processes). Credyx does not force-close Self-Service invoices.
