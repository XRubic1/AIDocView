# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** July 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how **Electro Pionir DOOEL**, doing business as **Credyx A/R Solutions** (“**Credyx**,” “**we**,” “**us**,” or “**our**”) collects, uses, shares, and protects information when you use our websites, waitlist, client portal, and related A/R back-office services (the “**Services**”).

Credyx provides accounts receivable and back-office support for trucking carriers. We are **not** a factor, lender, cash-advance provider, or freight broker.

Contact for privacy requests: **privacy@credyx.net**

---

## 1. Information we collect

### 1.1 Information you provide

- **Waitlist / marketing:** company name, contact name, email, phone, fleet size, how you heard about us, and optional notes.
- **Account & enrollment:** business and contact details needed to create a carrier account and communicate with your team.
- **Login credentials:** authentication data processed through our identity provider.
- **Setup / compliance documents** you upload to the document vault, which may include W-9, certificate of insurance (COI), MC/DOT authority documents, voided check or bank letter, and Notice of Assignment (NOA) when a factoring arrangement applies.
- **Operational documents & data:** rate confirmations, bills of lading, proofs of delivery, invoices, broker contacts, remittance instructions, payment records, holds/disputes notes, and related freight paperwork you or your team submit.
- **Support & communications:** messages you send to Credyx (email, portal, or assistant features).

### 1.2 Information we generate or receive in providing Services

- Invoice status, aging, collections activity, billing and fee records for Credyx’s platform charges.
- Broker responses and payment confirmations related to loads we handle for you.
- Technical logs needed to operate and secure the Services (e.g., timestamps, approximate device/browser data, IP address, error logs).

### 1.3 Cookies and similar technologies

We may use essential cookies or local storage required for login sessions and basic site function. On the waitlist / marketing site, we also collect **anonymous usage events** (for example page views, button clicks, and which sections were viewed) tied to a temporary session identifier stored in your browser, plus approximate **IP address and region** used to understand where traffic comes from, and whether the visit was from a **mobile or desktop** device. These events help us understand how visitors use the site. They do not include your name or email unless you submit the waitlist form separately.

## 2. How we use information

We use information to:

- Operate the waitlist and contact you about Credyx.
- Provide A/R services: receive and verify paperwork, prepare and submit invoice packets to brokers, follow up on holds/disputes, track payment status, and keep you informed.
- Communicate with brokers and payors **as your authorized back-office / A/R agent** (not as a purchaser of your invoices, unless a separate factoring arrangement exists between you and a third party).
- Bill you for Credyx platform and service fees and process related payment requests.
- Secure, troubleshoot, and improve the Services, including using automated and AI-assisted tools to classify, extract, or review document content for operational accuracy.
- Comply with law, enforce agreements, and protect Credyx, our users, and others.

We do **not** sell your personal information.

---

## 3. How we share information

We may share information with:

- **Service providers** who help us run the Services (for example hosting, authentication, email delivery, and payment processing), under obligations to protect the data.
- **Brokers, shippers, and payors** when needed to invoice, follow up, or confirm payment **on your behalf** as part of the Services you requested.
- **Your authorized users** on your carrier account.
- **Professional advisors** (legal, accounting) under confidentiality.
- **Authorities or other parties** when required by law, legal process, or to protect rights and safety.
- A **successor** in connection with a merger, acquisition, or asset sale, subject to appropriate protections.

We do not share your vault documents or freight data for unrelated marketing by third parties.

---

## 4. AI and document processing

Freight and setup documents may be processed with automated systems and AI-assisted review to classify pages, extract fields, flag missing items, and support human operators. Outputs are used to deliver the Services; humans remain involved in operational decisions as appropriate to the service plan (Managed or Self-Service).

Do not upload information you are not authorized to share with Credyx for these purposes.

## 5. Retention

We retain information for as long as needed to provide the Services, meet legal/accounting obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements. When you leave Credyx, we may retain limited records as required by law or legitimate business needs (for example billing history). You may request deletion as described below; we will honor requests where we are not required or permitted to keep the data.

We use administrative, technical, and organizational measures designed to protect information (access controls, encryption in transit where supported by our providers, and role-based access within Credyx). No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.
