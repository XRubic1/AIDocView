# Exhibit A — Fee Schedule

**Attached to the Carrier Master Service Agreement**

**Credyx:** as defined in the Carrier Master Service Agreement (Credyx A/R Solutions)  
**Client:** as identified in the MSA signature block  
**Effective with MSA dated:** July 29, 2026

This Exhibit A describes how Client pays Credyx. It does **not** describe broker freight payments. Brokers pay Client; Credyx bills Client for Credyx fees only.

---

## 1. Fee components

Client’s monthly Credyx bill includes only the fees **agreed** under this Exhibit (and any signed fee terms for the Client). No platform fee or collection fee applies unless agreed as described below.

1. **Platform (app) fee:** US $99 per month **if agreed** by the parties (for example by signing this Exhibit with the platform fee selected, confirming it at enrollment, or completing §10 / another writing). If no platform fee is agreed, none is charged.
2. **Collection / A/R service fee:** the **agreed** percentage or flat rate under §3 or §10, applied to **eligible collected amounts** in the billing period (see §2). If no collection fee is agreed, none is charged.

```
Monthly Credyx bill ≈ (agreed platform fee, if any) + (agreed rate × eligible collections, if any)
                     + any other adjustments the parties have agreed (e.g., force-close fee lines)
```

Credyx does not deduct its fee from broker remittances. Client pays Credyx separately via the payment method Credyx designates.

---

## 2. What the % applies to (eligible collections)

Unless otherwise agreed in writing:

- The percentage fee applies to **confirmed collections** attributable to Client invoices handled through Credyx in the billing month (payments Credyx records as confirmed).
- Pending, reversed, or uncollected amounts are not billed as collections.
- **Write-offs / uncollected balances** do not generate a collections percentage fee (Credyx is not paid a % on money never collected), subject to any force-close rule below.
- Only invoices/activity **eligible** under Client’s plan and Credyx’s billing rules are included. Credyx does not charge collection fees on amounts outside the Services.

If Client and Credyx agree a specific rate in §10 (or store agreed `fee_terms` on the Client account), that **agreed rate controls** for the covered activity.

---

## 3. Agreed collection fee schedule

By signing the MSA and this Exhibit (or by confirming fee terms at enrollment), Client and Credyx **agree** to the collection fees below **when a collection fee is part of the agreed arrangement**, unless a different rate is completed and initialed in §10.

If the parties agree there is **no** collection fee, §3 does not apply.

Rates (when agreed) are based on Client’s **collected volume in the billing month** (flat tier on the whole base for that month):

| Monthly collected volume (Client) | Agreed rate | Agreed factoring-related rate* |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Under US $100,000 | **1.25%** | **0.70%** |
| US $100,000 – $250,000 | **1.00%** | **0.60%** |
| US $250,000 – $500,000 | **0.90%** | **0.50%** |
| Over US $500,000 | As separately agreed in writing | As separately agreed in writing |

\*Factoring-related rates apply only when Credyx has marked the account as factoring-related (typically evidenced by a third-party NOA in the vault). Factoring is Client’s relationship with a third party — not Credyx buying invoices.

**Self-Service and Managed** use the same agreed schedule above unless the parties agree a different Managed premium or override in §10 or another writing.

These are **agreed contractual fees for this Client**, not a public “list price” Credyx must offer every carrier.

---

## 4. Trial

If Credyx grants a trial (`trial` subscription status):

- Default draft posture: **platform fee and collection fee waived** during the stated trial window, unless Credyx’s trial notice says otherwise.
- After trial, the **agreed** fees under this Exhibit apply automatically unless the account is cancelled or the parties agree otherwise in writing.

---

## 5. Managed force-close (if used)

Force-close eligibility and process are defined in the MSA (Managed plan; typically **90+ days outstanding** after collection efforts). If Credyx force-closes an eligible invoice/load, Credyx may add a one-time fee line of:

**agreed applicable rate × invoice amount**

for that item in the month’s bill (so work performed is not zero-fee solely because the broker never paid), unless the parties agree otherwise in writing. Force-close does not make Credyx liable for unpaid freight.

---

## 6. Credyx fee bills — invoicing and payment

This section covers **Client’s payment of Credyx fees only**. It does **not** set broker freight payment terms (see the MSA regarding per-broker due dates).

- Credyx issues periodic **Credyx fee bills** (typically monthly) summarizing agreed platform fees, collection fees, and adjustments.
- Payment of each Credyx fee bill is due on the **due date stated on that bill**.
- Launch payment method: payment requests (or successor method Credyx specifies).
- Client must keep billing contacts and payment details current.

---

## 7. Late payment, warning, and lock (Credyx fee bills)

Applies to unpaid **Credyx fee bills**, measured from the bill’s stated due date (or bill date if no separate due date is shown):

| Timing | Consequence (draft product rule) |
| --- | --- |
| Credyx fee bill issued | Payment request sent |
| ~30 days past due | Reminder |
| ~60 days past due | Stronger portal warning of upcoming lock |
| **~90 days past due** | **Account locked / suspended** — portal limited to billing resolution until settled |

Locking does not forgive amounts owed. Credyx may reactivate after confirmed payment or admin grace, at Credyx’s discretion.

These timelines are about paying Credyx — **not** about when brokers must pay Client’s freight invoices.

---

## 8. Fee snapshots

Where Credyx locks fee terms to a batch or invoice, billing for that activity uses the **locked snapshot**, not a later rate change. Future rate changes apply going forward only as described in the MSA and only for periods after notice, unless the parties agree otherwise.

---

## 9. Taxes

Fees are exclusive of applicable taxes. Client is responsible for taxes imposed on Client’s business; Credyx may charge tax if legally required to collect it.
