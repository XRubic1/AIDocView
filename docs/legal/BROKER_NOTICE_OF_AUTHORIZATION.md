The Carrier named above (the “**Carrier**”) agrees and confirms that **Credyx A/R Solutions** is authorized to act as Carrier’s accounts-receivable and back-office agent in dealings with you (the broker, shipper, payor, or your AP contacts).

---

## 1. What Credyx is authorized to do

Carrier agrees and confirms that Credyx may, on Carrier’s behalf:

1. **Send documents** and invoice packets (including supporting paperwork such as rate confirmations, BOLs, PODs, and related load documents) to you.
2. **Submit invoices** for payment for loads handled through Credyx.
3. **Confirm statuses** — request and receive updates on invoice acceptance, document status, holds, and payment status.
4. **Follow up** on any of the following related to Carrier’s invoices:
   - disputes and holds;
   - short payments;
   - claims;
   - overpayments; and
   - other payment or paperwork issues that affect settlement of the invoice.
5. **Provide remittance information** supplied by Carrier (see Section 3) so you know where payment must be sent.
6. Keep Carrier informed of material developments known to Credyx.

You may rely on communications from Credyx regarding the above as communications authorized by Carrier.

---

## 2. Documents and invoices — what Credyx may and may not change

### 2.1 Source paperwork — no substantive changes

Credyx will **not** change the substance of Carrier’s source paperwork (for example Rate Confirmations, Bills of Lading, Proofs of Delivery, or similar load documents).

Automated or AI-assisted tools **may** be used only to enhance **visibility / readability** of documents when needed (for example clearer BOL images for review). Such tools will **not** be used to alter the substantive content of those source documents.

### 2.2 Invoices — billing accuracy only

Credyx **may** prepare, edit, or adjust **invoices** solely to ensure correct billing and that accurate information is listed for payment (for example amounts, references, dates, load identifiers, and required billing fields consistent with Carrier’s paperwork and instructions).

---

## 3. Remittance and where payment must go

### 3.1 Payments go to Carrier (or Carrier’s factor) — never to Credyx

- **All freight payments must be made to the Carrier** (or, if applicable, to Carrier’s factoring company under a valid NOA), **not to Credyx**.
- **Do not remit freight payment to Credyx under any circumstances** — including if Credyx submits the invoice, follows up on status, or attaches remittance materials.
- Credyx does **not** receive, hold, or deposit freight payments from brokers/payors as payment of Carrier’s freight.

### 3.2 Remittance provided at Carrier’s request

- Remittance instructions and banking evidence are provided **by Carrier** (or at Carrier’s request through Credyx).
- Credyx will **include** Carrier-approved remittance information with invoice packets as directed by Carrier.
- Credyx does **not** invent or substitute its own remittance / pay-to instructions in place of Carrier’s.

### 3.3 Every invoice packet includes remittance

Every invoice (or invoice packet) submitted by Credyx on Carrier’s behalf will include **attached remittance information** showing where payment must be sent (for example remittance advice, bank letter, and/or voided check, as supplied or approved by Carrier).

### 3.4 Factoring / Notice of Assignment (NOA)

If Carrier is under a factoring agreement, invoice packets will include the applicable **Notice of Assignment (NOA)** for the factoring company. In that case, pay according to the NOA (to the factor named therein), **not** to Credyx.

## 4. How to treat this notice

- This notice confirms Carrier’s authorization for Credyx to perform the activities in Section 1.
- It does **not** make Credyx a party to your carrier–broker agreement, and it does **not** transfer ownership of invoices to Credyx.
- Fee arrangements between Credyx and Carrier are separate; Credyx invoices **Carrier** for Credyx’s own service fees — those fees are **not** freight amounts you owe Credyx.
