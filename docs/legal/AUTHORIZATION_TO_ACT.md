# Exhibit B — Authorization to Act

**Attached to the Carrier Master Service Agreement**

**Client** authorizes **Credyx A/R Solutions** (“**Credyx**”), as defined in the Carrier Master Service Agreement, to act as Client’s accounts-receivable and back-office agent as follows.

---

## 1. Authority granted

Client authorizes Credyx to, on Client’s behalf:

1. Prepare and submit invoice packets and supporting paperwork to brokers, shippers, payors, and their AP contacts.
2. Communicate by email, phone, portal, or other reasonable means regarding invoice acceptance, document deficiencies, holds, disputes, and payment status.
3. Provide Client’s remittance instructions and include Client-supplied banking evidence (such as a voided check or bank letter) with invoice packets so payment is directed to **Client**.
4. Request status updates and payment information related to Client’s invoices handled through Credyx.
5. Keep Client informed of material holds, disputes, and payment confirmations known to Credyx.

---

## 2. Limits of authority

This authorization does **not** authorize Credyx to:

- purchase, factor, or take assignment of Client’s invoices;
- receive or deposit freight payments from brokers into Credyx-controlled accounts as payment of Client’s freight (unless a separate written escrow/instruction expressly says otherwise — default is **none**);
- modify Rate Confirmations, Bills of Lading, PODs, or other source document substance (readability enhancement and data extraction only, per the MSA);
- change Client’s bank letter, voided check, or remittance instructions;
- bind Client to new freight contracts or carrier-broker agreements; or
- act as Client’s attorney-at-law.

---

## 3. Client representations

Client represents that:

- Client owns the receivables and has authority to invoice the applicable brokers/payors (or is otherwise authorized to have Credyx submit on Client’s behalf).
- Documents and remittance materials Client provides are accurate and lawfully usable for these purposes.
- If a third-party factor holds rights under an NOA, Client has disclosed that arrangement and provided required NOA documentation.

---

## 4. Duration

This authorization starts on the MSA Effective Date and continues until the MSA terminates, or until Client revokes it in writing (email sufficient). Revocation may limit or end Credyx’s ability to perform Managed or invoicing Services. Fees already incurred remain payable.

---

## 5. Acknowledgement

Client acknowledges brokers may rely on this authorization and on remittance materials supplied by Client. Credyx’s fee relationship remains solely between Credyx and Client under Exhibit A.
