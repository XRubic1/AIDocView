# Credyx caller sheet

**How to approach carriers on the phone** · Internal · September 2026

> Goal of every cold call: a 15-minute demo, or permission to text a one-pager with a booked callback. Do not onboard on the first call.

## What we sell

Credyx is a **back office for small carriers**. After they deliver, they send the rate con, BOL and POD. We verify the paperwork, invoice the broker, follow up until they get paid, and they see every invoice on their phone.

**Not factoring.** We do not buy invoices, do not advance money, and never touch the freight payment. The broker pays them. We bill a small % after the money arrives.

Lead with **Managed**: "We do it for you." Self-Service only if they already have a bookkeeper who just wants the portal.

Never say we confirm delivery, capture POD, get paid faster, instant cash, or guarantee a pay date.

## Who to call

**Primary:** 1–15 trucks, no factor — owner bills, or a spouse / dispatcher does it at night.

**Secondary:** they factor and are tired of the fee. Keep the factor if they need cash; we work alongside them at a lower rate.

**Not the main target:** happy factored carriers. Their factor already invoices and collects.

Call Tuesday–Thursday, 9–11am and 2–4pm in their time zone. If they are driving, lock a callback. Do not pitch someone who said they are busy.

## The call

**1. Opener (10 seconds)**

```
Hi, is this [Name]? This is [Caller] with Credyx. I'll be quick — I know you're probably driving.
We do the billing and paperwork for small carriers. Can I ask you one question, and if it's not for you, I'll let you go?
```

If busy: "When's a better time today or tomorrow? Two minutes max." Lock it.

**2. Discovery — this decides the pitch**

```
Who handles your broker invoicing right now — you, somebody in the office, or a factoring company?
```

Then two of: How many trucks? How long after delivery does the invoice go out? What happens when a broker sits on one? What's the most annoying part of the paperwork? Listen for Sunday-night billing, lost PODs, unpaid detention, a broker 60 days out, "my wife does it."

**3. Pitch — pick one**

They bill themselves (or family):

```
After you deliver, you send us the rate con, BOL and POD — photo, email, or upload.
We check the paperwork, invoice the broker, and stay on them until they pay you.
You see every invoice on your phone — submitted, invoiced, paid, or on hold.
The broker pays you directly. We never touch your money. This isn't factoring.
```

They have a factor:

```
Keep them if you need the money fast. We work alongside factors.
They check paperwork to protect their advance. When a broker short-pays or disputes, it comes back as a chargeback.
We chase those, we handle brokers they won't buy, and you see every invoice in one place.
Because they fund, our rate is lower — well under one percent.
```

Ask: "Does your factor ever decline a broker? What do you do with those?"

Bookkeeper / dispatcher bills: "You don't need to replace them — they need to stop chasing brokers. We take invoicing and follow-up. You both see every load in one portal."

**4. Price — say it once, then stop**

```
It's a percentage of what we collect — about one and a quarter percent for a fleet your size, charged after the broker pays you. Nothing upfront.
If a broker never pays and the invoice is written off, there's no percentage on it.
First month: any invoice you submit has no fee.
```

With a factor on file: **0.70%**. Do not invent a rate. Do not discount on the first call.

**5. Close — pick one**

```
I can do a 15-minute screen share and show you what you'd see on your phone — or I text you a one-pager and call you back Thursday. Which is easier?
```

Write down: name, cell, email, trucks, who bills, factor Y/N, next step and date. If no: "Mind if I check back in a couple of months?" If they say don't call again, never call again.

## Factoring, if they ask

Factoring is **cash today**. Credyx is **the back office**. Different products.

Factor owns the invoice (NOA). Broker pays the factor. Typical cost 2.5–3.5% plus reserves. Recourse: if the broker hasn't paid in 60–90 days, they claw the advance back. Credyx: they keep the invoice, broker pays them, about 1% after they're paid, 30 days' notice, nothing to unwind.

Never say factors don't check paperwork or don't collect — they do, for their own invoice. Never say "100% integration." Say: we keep their NOA on file, send packets the way the factor needs them, and our rate drops. If they only need cash sometimes: broker quick-pay on those loads + Credyx on everything is often cheaper than factoring 100% of volume.

## Rules

**Never say:** get paid faster · same-day / instant cash · guaranteed pay date · best price on the market · unbelievable offer · factors don't verify docs · 100% factor integration · we confirm delivery / capture POD · any rate not on the table.

**Always:** "We are not a factoring company." Say "charged after you're paid" with every number. End with a demo, a callback, or a clean no. Ask before you text. Honor do-not-call.

**After the call:** text the one-pager the same hour if they said yes. Demo shows Submitted → Verified → Invoiced → Paid, the vault, and a hold — demo data only. They will need: W-9 · COI · MC/DOT · voided check or bank letter · NOA if they factor · signed MSA + Fee Schedule.
