# Sales pay and year-1 forecast

**Commission-only · First 20 clients · 40% of Credyx fees** · Internal · September 2026

> No salary. No weekly draw. No guarantee. If you do not close a paying client, you take home **$0**. The numbers below are a forecast, not a promise.

## The deal

You get **40% of what Credyx bills** on the first **20 clients you personally close**. Credyx bills about **1%** of the money the carrier actually collected that month. So your cut is **0.40% of their collected freight**.

Example, one client, one month (after the free month):

| | Amount |
| --- | --- |
| Carrier collected from brokers | $50,000 |
| Credyx bills the carrier (1%) | $500 |
| You take home (40% of $500) | **$200** |
| Credyx keeps (to run the back office) | $300 |

**Rules that decide whether you get paid**

- Paid only on **confirmed collections**. An invoice sitting unpaid is $0 for Credyx and $0 for you.
- **First month is free** for the carrier. You earn **$0** on invoices they submit in that window.
- **Factored** clients pay Credyx 0.70%, not 1%. Your 40% of that is **0.28%** of collections — a smaller check.
- Written-off / never-collected invoices: no Credyx fee, **no commission**.
- After client 20, this 40% deal stops for new logos. What happens next is a separate conversation.
- This forecast assumes you keep 40% on those clients **through the end of year 1** while they stay and pay. Year 2 is not promised here.

You do not get a cut of freight. You get a cut of **Credyx's fee**, after the broker has paid the carrier and Credyx has billed.

## What one client is worth

Primary target is 1–8 trucks doing their own billing. Do not plan your year on 15-truck fleets. Those are rare on a cold-call list.

| Fleet | Typical monthly collections | Credyx fee (1%) | Your 40% / month | If they stay 11 paying months |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 truck | $20,000 | $200 | **$80** | $880 |
| 2–3 trucks | $50,000 | $500 | **$200** | $2,200 |
| 5–6 trucks | $90,000 | $900 | **$360** | $3,960 |
| 8–10 trucks | $140,000 | $1,400 | **$560** | $6,160 |
| Same 2–3 trucks, **factored** | $50,000 | $350 (0.70%) | **$140** | $1,540 |

**Use $200 / month as your planning unit.** That is a 2–3 truck carrier, no factor, after the free month. Most of your first closes will look like this, not the $560 row.

A client who onboards in November is worth **one check** this year, not $2,200. Timing is the whole game.

## What a beginner year actually looks like

Credyx is new. You have no brand, no inbound, no warm list. Cold calls to owner-operators. The first 8–10 weeks you are building pipeline. **Budget $0 for months 1–2.** That is normal, not a failure.

If you work the caller sheet (80–100 dials a day, Tue–Thu plus callbacks): about **1 paying onboard every 4–6 weeks** once you can run the script. Twenty clients in year 1 is a stretch, not the base case. People who treat 20 as "the plan" quit in month 3 when the bank account is still empty.

**Assumptions behind the four scenarios**

- Average new client: **$50,000 / month collected**, 1% fee, you get $200 / paying month
- Free month on every new account
- First close no earlier than month 3 (realistic) or month 2 (strong)
- About **1 in 5** clients is gone before month 12 (quit trucking, go back to a factor, stop sending docs)
- Commission hits the month after they start paying Credyx — there is a lag. The tables already bake that in by starting pay the month after onboard.

| Scenario | Clients closed in year 1 | What it takes | Year-1 take-home |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Miss** | 0–2 | Inconsistent dials, or they never leave factoring | **$0 – $2,400** |
| **Base (plan for this)** | 6 | Daily dials, demos booked, 6 small fleets staggered | **~$6,000 – $8,000** |
| **Strong** | 12 | Full days on the phone, tight follow-up, a couple of 5-truck accounts | **~$10,000 – $14,000** |
| **Stretch (the 20-client cap)** | 20 | Almost 2 closes a month after month 2. Unusual for a new brand. | **~$18,000 – $24,000** |

Do **not** tell yourself "20 clients × $200 × 12 = $48,000." That number is a **full book, already ramped, all still paying**. You will not have that in month 12 of a from-scratch year.

## Base case — month by month

Six clients. First close in month 3, then roughly one every 6 weeks. Mix is all ~$50k / month. One client (the second) churns after 5 paying months.

| Month | New clients | Paying clients that month | Your check |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 0 | 0 | **$0** |
| 2 | 0 | 0 | **$0** |
| 3 | 1 (trial) | 0 | **$0** |
| 4 | 0 | 1 | **$200** |
| 5 | 1 (trial) | 1 | **$200** |
| 6 | 1 (trial) | 2 | **$400** |
| 7 | 0 | 3 | **$600** |
| 8 | 1 (trial) | 3 | **$600** |
| 9 | 1 (trial) | 3 (one churned) | **$600** |
| 10 | 0 | 4 | **$800** |
| 11 | 1 (trial) | 4 | **$800** |
| 12 | 0 | 5 | **$1,000** |
| **Year 1** | **6 closed** | — | **$5,200** |

If two of those six are 5–6 truck fleets ($360 / month instead of $200), year 1 lands closer to **$7,500**. If two are factored, it lands closer to **$4,500**. The book you close matters more than the close count.

**What "full book" looks like — year 2, if they stay**

If you somehow hold 16 of 20 clients at $50k into a second year, and the 40% still applies: 16 × $200 = **$3,200 / month**, about **$38,000 / year**. That is a mature portfolio, not year 1. Do not spend it before you have it.

## How to get to the base case

The paycheck follows **paying clients**, not dials. But without volume you will sit on $0.

| Activity | Weekly habit that supports 6 clients / year |
| --- | --- |
| Dials | 80–100 per day, 4 days a week. Below 50/day you will miss. |
| Demos booked | 3–5 real screen-shares a week by month 3 |
| Follow-up | Every lead gets a callback time or it is dead |
| Who you call | 1–8 trucks, no factor, authority 6–24 months old |
| Who you skip chasing | Happy factored carriers. Low commission, hard close. |

One 6-truck client is worth more than two 1-truck owners who disappear. Qualify trucks and "who bills" in the first minute. Do not spend a month babysitting a one-truck maybe.

## Read this twice

- **Months 1–2 will be $0** if you are doing this from a cold list. If you cannot float that, this deal will break you. That is the honest part of commission-only.
- You get paid **after they get paid**, then after Credyx bills. A "closed" client who never sends docs is not a client.
- Twenty clients is the **cap**, not the forecast. Plan your rent on the base case.
- Inflating a close ("they'll start next month") does not create a check. Collections do.
